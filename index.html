Permissions

The Game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. Google Play and the Android operating system may process data under Google’s own privacy policy when you download or use apps; this is outside my control.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. Since no personal data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.
